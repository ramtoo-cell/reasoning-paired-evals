# Data card

- Source: hand-authored synthetic examples created with AI assistance for this repository.
- Real people, customer data, and model outputs: none.
- Intended use: local demonstrations and regression tests.
- Evaluation validity: not a benchmark and not evidence of model performance.
- Coverage: small illustrative cases only; see tests for additional edge cases.
- Before real use: document collection, consent/licensing where relevant, labeling, splits, exclusions, and known biases.
